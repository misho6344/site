Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NoAdFrame ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit or use https://noadframe.onrender.com/ (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: if you register, we process the username, email address, password credential (stored as a one-way password hash), and account creation information needed to provide your account.
  • User content: comments and other information you choose to submit through the Service.
  • Technical information: our hosting and security providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser/device details, timestamps, and security or error logs to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: the Service uses cookies needed for account sessions and request security, including session and CSRF-protection cookies. These support login and site security.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate and maintain the Service, create and authenticate accounts, display and manage comments, provide account features, protect against abuse and unauthorized access, respond to requests, and diagnose technical problems. We do not sell personal information.

3. Optional TikTok connection

If an authorized NoAdFrame administrator chooses to connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we may receive and process the TikTok account identifier and profile information made available by the permissions granted. If the administrator grants the video-list permission, we may retrieve the account's public video information and available engagement metrics to provide the Service's administrative trailer/video statistics features.

TikTok authorization is optional. We use the access only for the feature the administrator enables. OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted form for account connection and authorized API access. We do not ask users to provide their TikTok password to NoAdFrame. TikTok handles the login and authorization process under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. The administrator can disconnect the TikTok account through the NoAdFrame admin panel; disconnection stops future API access through that connection and initiates removal of the locally stored connection credentials. TikTok may continue to retain information under its own policies.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers that help us host, secure, store, and operate the Service, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, users, and the Service. Such providers process information for the services they provide.

5. Data retention and security

We retain account information and user content while needed to provide the Service and for legitimate security, operational, and legal purposes. Technical logs are retained according to operational needs and provider settings. We use reasonable safeguards, including password hashing, session security controls, and encryption for stored TikTok authorization tokens. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may stop using the Service and, where account features are available, sign out or manage your account. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us through the contact channel published on the NoAdFrame website. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited information where required for security or legal obligations.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us through the website's published contact channel.

8. Third-party services and links

The Service may link to or embed content from third-party services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact channel published on the NoAdFrame website: https://noadframe.onrender.com/.
